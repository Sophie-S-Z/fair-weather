# ADR 0012: Part 1 rules for chapters 4 to 10

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

Milestone M2 builds chapters 4 to 10, the five interludes, and the myth. The spec names these features but leaves gaps: M1 starts every chapter from a fresh world, so nothing the village learned survives a chapter; lanterns, bridges, the ridge, ambient sky bids, the mushroom's taker, and the festival's spread have no exact rule; the tool tray holds three tools while P1 says a verb never goes away; and T6 asks for 1,000 random-play runs, which the full sim cannot do in a test run.

## Decision

1. **The year carries from chapter 4 on.** A chapter config with `carry: true` starts from the last snapshot of the chapter before it. For each creature in the base scenario it takes the dials, the shell (which becomes the new resting shell, so a body keeps its history), the sky warmth, an `away` state, `gone` (a creature over the ridge stays gone), and its diary (its last 20 events, spec 9.1), plus both warmths of every tie between base creatures. Slights, berries, positions, and newcomers do not carry. Chapters 0 to 3 keep `carry: false` and start fresh, as approved in M1. A skipped chapter carries the world as it was when the player skipped. T3 for a carried chapter starts from a stored carry made by one seeded headless run of the chapters before it.
2. **The snapshot grows** by what the new views and checks read: each creature's dials, `dancing`, and `gone`, every tie's two warmths, the latest rumor with the ids that hold it, the water rectangles, and the mushroom. A story valley has at most 435 ties, so a snapshot stays small.
3. **Land.** Water is a list of rectangles in the scenario. A bridge's deck crosses its water rectangle the short way and is `2 × bridge.radius` (64) wide along the water; the sim and the Meadow use this one rectangle (`bridgeDeck`, sent to render in the snapshot). Each water rectangle has a bank (`bank.side` 52 left, right and above, `bank.below` 128 under it, sized from the largest drawn bodies and their feet shadows), so no drawn body covers water: a creature's feet enter a bank only on a deck, which reaches `bridge.landing` (14) past the bank each side, and a creature that starts in a bank off every deck (the chapter 6 islet's) is held only by the water itself. A blocked creature heads for the nearest bridge on that water, or picks a new goal when there is none. A bridge is placed only inside water. A lantern scales the fog at any point within `lantern.radius` (120) by `lantern.fogFactor` (0.5), as the Lantern-bearer knack does. The thaw brook runs in chapters 9 and 10 only. A scenario `ridgeX` is the east ridge: a wandering creature at or past it leaves for good (`gone`, it never returns in Part 1), random goals stay west of it, and a gust sets each creature it pushes to walk to the gust's end point. So only the player's wind sends anyone over. A refused creature heads for the bridge's foot: the end of its deck, just past the bank, on its own side, or on its goal's side when it stands on the deck. When no bridge can help, because there is none on that water or the creature already heads for the foot or stands within `move.arriveRadius` of it, it marks the refused goal's cell as visited as often as its most visited cell and picks a random least-visited cell. With a ridge, every goal except a gust's is capped 18 units west of it (`move.arriveRadius` plus the stopping distance `speed² / (2 × accel)`), so a creature never drifts over while it turns.
4. **Ambient sky bids.** In a scenario with `ambientSkyBids`, at each dusk, with chance `sky.ambientBidChance` (0.5), the awake creature with the highest sky warmth (a tie goes to the lower id) bids to the sky. The director answers `away` after 6 seconds, as for a story bid, but does not prompt it again.
5. **The dew mushroom** follows spec 6.2 for the next dawn's drops. One drop is one berry. A creature within `meet.radius` takes at most one drop per day, and only when it carries fewer than `mushroom.takeBelowBerries` (1) berries, or carries fewer than `forage.carryMax` while it has fewer than `mushroom.trustFriends` (2) friends. Distrust hoards; friendship leaves drops for others. A withered mushroom gives 0 drops at the next 3 dawns and 10 at the fourth.
6. **The festival.** A story beat starts the dance with a `dance` command (Pim, who carries Wisp's seed). Every `festival.everyTicks` (10) ticks, every awake creature that is not dancing starts when at least its threshold of friends dance, all at once from the same count. The threshold is `festival.threshold` (1, so one seeded dancer can start a friend; at 2 the dance could never spread from Pim, M2.9), and Bell's is 3 (spec 5.3). Dancing changes nothing else.
7. **Old Moss's grudge** is against Krit: last winter a hungry Krit kept Moss's gift. Chapter 8 starts that tie side with 1 slight.
8. **The tool tray.** Spec 12.2's limit of three tools wins over P1. Each chapter lists at most three world tools. A taught verb stays part of the game and comes back in any later chapter that lists it. Bet, listen, and the mind verbs (peek, zoom, rewind) are never tray tools.
9. **Interludes** are chapter configs with `kind: "interlude"` and an `activity`. They run the sim, so the carry passes through them, with a 600-second day, so no dusk passes during one.
10. **Rewind is a reading tool.** It shows the world at an earlier tick of the chapter and never changes the live world. The worker rebuilds that tick from the chapter's first world or a dusk copy, plus the accepted commands. After a worker restart, the earliest tick it can show is the checkpoint it restarted from.
11. **T6 uses synthetic random play.** A seeded generator writes 1,000 random year logs in the runner's log format. The myth must differ in at least 900. A separate test feeds a real headless year log through the myth to check the two formats agree.
12. **The Quiet Visitor is drawn, not simulated.** Three minutes of real time with no input in one scene brings it, once per scene. It never enters the sim, so it cannot change a hash. Its feather is counted in the profile store with the *Book of Skies*.
13. **A margin note** has a title, the science, its limits, the strongest counter-view, an open question, and at least one source (spec 10.5).
14. **Bets on a number** list their options from low to high with `buckets`, one fewer than the options. The answer is the option whose bucket holds the measured value.
15. **Chapter calibration (M2.8).** Each of chapters 4 to 7 meets two checks over 200 seeds from the stored carry: the T3 player plays the close call in fewer than 10 runs, and a control with the same seeds, where the player never uses the chapter's verb, fires the twist in at most 20 runs of the first window (the ch3-control form). One sim rule was added, behind an optional scenario field: with `rumorFirst`, a creature that holds the latest rumor shares it at dusk before its strongest opinion (spec 9.4 otherwise shares only the strongest, so a 0.6 rumor could not pass through warm carried ties). No draw is added, and the M0 scenario lacks the field, so the golden hash cannot move. A chapter's dial overrides, its own and its chosen variant's, apply to that chapter only: the carry it leaves puts each overridden dial back to the value the chapter received, and every other dial carries its end value, so imitation and gust shifts stay history. A flag read from a dial (`echoForgiveness`) takes the same restored value. Chapter 4: the twist window opens on the first shine. No one shares on a first meeting (Opener 0 for all) and Krit and every other creature hold a 4-slight grudge both ways, so the village keeps to itself; light on Krit's keeps spreads them to the meetings nearby, where a give meets a keep and shells tighten. Chapter 5: `rumorFirst`, Ear 0.1 for all but Krit, and the rumor reaches Echo at 38 s, after her first meetings of the day (the plan said 2 s, but then Echo's first meeting with Krit set the rumor right in about 13% of seeds); the control cuts Echo's threads. Chapter 6: Echo and Krit start on a small islet by the crooked tree (water all round, one bush) and every creature has Forgiveness 0 and Memory 0, so each gives as the other gave last, and Krit has Opener 0 so Echo bids; the fog makes the first misread, and the twist window opens on the first fog.
16. **Chapters 8 and 9 (M2.9)** meet the item 15 checks, with the control from an empty carry (no ties, base dials), since neither chapter has a verb of its own: spec 6.2 says earlier choices decide both. Chapter 8: Old Moss is a Grudger (Forgiveness 0, Memory 1, spec 9.3), so under the spec 9.3 gift rule its one slight against Krit fades only after about 30 days, and a culture of forgiving friends could change Moss only through imitation, at about 6% a copy; the first build played the close call in 27 of 40 seeds. So one sim rule was added, behind an optional scenario field: with `friendsForgive`, a giver with at least `mushroom.trustFriends` (2) mutual friends (each side's warmth above `friend.threshold`) weighs remembered slights against the higher of its own Forgiveness and their mean Forgiveness. Every base Forgiveness but Moss's is at least 0.2, and one slight is forgiven at 1/6, so in practice the rule counts mutual friendships: two mutual friends carry Moss. It draws nothing, and the M0 scenario lacks the field, so the golden hash cannot move. Counting every friend, or one mutual friend, let a village of strangers carry Moss in 73 and 42 of 200 controls; two mutual friends, the winter trust count of item 5, brings it to 20 of seeds 1 to 200 and 14 of seeds 201 to 400, so 34 of 400 (about 8.5%). The control test's limit of 20 is therefore at the high tail of that rate: a later change that moves ch8's draws can push it over without being a new bug. The P6 variant (`litConflict` at least 0.5 and `rumorStuck`, the path the scripted year takes) sends Krit away; Krit comes back only by the weekly return (spec 9.4), and a retry cannot arrange a meeting with an away creature. That close call is the earned "ache" of earlier choices and is exempt from the fewer-than-10 limit: over 200 seeds from the stored carry it plays in 84 (retries 0:5, 1:50, 2:32, 3:113), and its test only checks that it stays the minority outcome. The close-call line says only that winter ended without Moss giving Krit a drop, which holds on both paths. Chapter 9: one dancer cannot start anyone at threshold 2, so the festival could never spread from Pim's seed; `festival.threshold` is now 1 (item 6) and chapter 9 sets only Bell's 3. The spread follows friendships and not positions, so the bridges do not decide it: from any year with friendships Pim's friend component dances and Bell, with three friends in it, dances too (200 of 200 from the stored carry, at the first spread after the seed); only a year without friendships (the empty-carry control, 4 of 200) leaves Bell still. From chapter 8 on, `gone: ["wisp"]` (and in interlude 7) is an authored departure after a chapter 7 close call: `wispPushed` keeps the sim result, and interlude 7 then says Wisp went over the ridge on its own.

## Consequences

- Winter, the festival, and the narrator rest on what the player built, so the "earlier choices decide" twists are real sim results.
- Every new sim rule is switched on by an optional scenario field, so the M0 golden hash `c29d2fa4dbda4dcf` does not change.
- When a chapter's content changes, the stored carries for the chapters after it are made again with `vitest -u`.
