# ADR 0015: Stage 2 rules for three valleys, rule cards, travelers, and the remix tree

**Status:** Accepted. Items 14 to 16 hold the calibration of the content tasks (M5.6 to M5.8).

## Context

Milestone M5 builds Stage 2 (spec 21.2): Foglight Heights, Toadstool Council, and Lily Shallows between the Stage 1 valleys, rule cards, travelers by code, and a remix tree for seed links. Spec 5.5, 7.3, 8, 9.5, 13.1, and 18 (T3, T5) name these features but leave gaps: how a snail climbs in a world where creatures walk to points, what stops the hero from walking down, how Brume comes along, who votes and when, what a ballot is under each rule, how a frog chooses a pad, what a rule card is, what a traveler carries, and how a link knows its parents when the game has no server. ADR 0007 placed the Foglight height map in `content/valleys/foglight/height.json`; item 3 here moves it into the scenario. ADRs 0013 and 0014 still hold; this ADR adds to them and wins where they differ.

## Decision

1. **The journey has six valleys.** The play order is spec 7.3's: Murmuration Fen, Hearsay Hollow, Foglight Heights, Toadstool Council, Lily Shallows, Halcyon, then the homecoming. Kith's silent run (ADR 0013 item 10) splits `kith.days` (20) over the six in play order, the remainder first: 4, 4, 3, 3, 3, 3. Wisp reads Kith's average after the first five. The stored homecoming carry is made again with the six legs, each nudged by the T3 player's first postcard of that valley.
2. **Three more pack questions (ADR 0013 item 4).** A pack may also answer: `steered(world, c)`, true when the pack sets `c`'s goal (then movement never picks a goal for it and it never leaves the lens); `mayStep(world, c, x, y, nx, ny, t)`, false when the pack refuses `c`'s step (the hero then stops, as at water); and `speedFactor(world, c, t)`, a factor on top speed. Without a pack each gives the plain rule (false, true, 1) and nothing draws.
3. **Foglight Heights (spec 9.5).** The scenario's pack holds the height map: `heights`, one integer from 0 to 9 per `foglight.cell` (100) square cell in row-major order (16 × 9 for the 1600 × 900 scene), `meadow` (the least height of the high meadow), and `guide` (Brume). Every snail (species `snail`) is steered. Every `foglight.everyTicks` (10) ticks, each snail not away and not following, in creature order, looks at the cells within `foglight.sight` (1) steps of its own (a square ring, scene cells only): when the highest of them is higher than its own cell, its goal is that cell's center plus its own offset (`OFFSETS[i mod 9]`, i its index among the snails); otherwise its goal is where it stands, and it stops. A tie goes to its own cell, then to the lower row-major index. At setup each fog cell (`fog.cellSize`) takes `max(base, clamp((foglight.fogBelow − h) / foglight.fogBelow, 0, 1))` (`fogBelow` 5), with h the height under its center, so low ground is foggy and the meadow is clear. The hero may not take a step that ends in a lower cell than the one it stands in (`mayStep`) unless `stepDown` is on; the `stepDown` local verb sets it on or off. When the hero steps down within `foglight.followRadii` (2) meeting radii of the guide while the guide's warmth toward the hero is at least `friend.threshold`, the guide follows (event `follow`): from then on it is steered to the hero's spot each tick, it climbs no more, and the hero's top speed is × `foglight.carrySpeed` (0.5) while the guide is within `foglight.followRadii` meeting radii (spec 7.3: "Brume asks to come along, which halves the hero's speed"). The twist `brumeClimbs`: the guide stands in a cell of height `meadow` or more. Knacks: fog (item 3 of ADR 0013) makes the Lantern-bearer and the Glassling the clear readers; a Kite-rider "sees one ridge ahead": the render's fog veil around the hero opens `foglight.kiteSight` (2) steps, not 1. The snapshot carries the map, the guide's state, and `stepDown`.
4. **Toadstool Council (spec 9.5).** Toadstools (species `toadstool`) are steered and stand where they are: they never walk. The scenario's pack holds four `spots`, each with an id, a scene point, and a point on the preference map (`wet`, `far`, each 0 to 1), and each voter's preference point in `prefs` (the 25 toadstools, Ambry the clerk among them). A voter's utility for a spot is `1 − d / √2`, d the distance between the two map points. The hero votes with `vote` (a spot id), which makes its ballot the one of a voter whose preference point is that spot's; a hero that never votes casts none. At the last tick of each day the council votes under its rule. **Plurality:** one vote for the highest utility. **Approval:** a vote for every spot whose utility is at least the voter's mean over the four. **Instant runoff:** voters rank spots by utility; each round removes the spot with the fewest first choices left (a tie removes the later spot) until one remains. **Score:** each voter gives each spot its utility; the highest sum wins. Any other tie goes to the earlier spot. A voter's tie in utility ranks the earlier spot first. The cloud goes to the winner: every bush within `rain.radius` of its scene point but a Seedcarrier's gift gains `rain.berries` up to `bush.limit` (the sandbox rain, no draw). Event `tally` (rule, winner, `glad`: the voters, not the hero, whose utility for the winner is above their mean). `propose` (approval, runoff, or score) works once: the next tally uses it. At that tally the council also counts plurality's winner on the same ballots; when the new winner's `glad` is greater, every voter's warmth toward the hero rises by `council.warmth` (0.1), otherwise it falls by 0.1, each × `knack.glassWarmth` for a Glassling ("locals see the hero's mood"), with an `opinion` event each and one `heard` event (rule, change). The twist `ruleHeard` is the `heard` event. The first tally is plurality's.
5. **Lily Shallows (spec 9.5).** Frogs (species `frog`) are steered. The scenario's pack holds six `pads` (an id and a scene point), each frog's pad in `padOf`, and each frog's songs in `songs` (each from `SONGS`: `reed`, `rain`, `old`). A pad holds at most `shallows.padCap` (8) frogs; a frog's goal is its pad's point plus the offset of its slot (the lowest free of 8, `SLOTS`). Every `shallows.everyTicks` (20) ticks, a frog with pad-mates (frogs on its pad, not away, not itself) is unhappy when its match share (the pad-mates who share any song with it, over its pad-mates) is below `shallows.minShare` (1/3), or, once the variety rule is on, above `shallows.maxShare` (0.9). The unhappy frogs, found before any hop, hop in creature order: each draws once and takes the pad at `floor(draw × n)` among the n other pads with room, in pad order; with none it stays. `learn` (a frog within `shallows.reachRadii` (2) meeting radii) sets the hero's song to that frog's first song; `sing` teaches the hero's song to a frog within reach that knows one song, not that one, at most once a day (event `song`). When `shallows.varietyAt` (3) frogs know two songs, the variety rule turns on (event `variety`). The first check at which no frog is unhappy gives the event `settled` once. The `sorting` metric is the mean, over frogs that know one song and have pad-mates, of the share of pad-mates who know that song. The twist `pondMixes`: the variety rule is on and `sorting` is at most `shallows.mixedAt`. The hero has no pad: it walks the pond's edge and counts in no share. Spec 19.2: songs are chosen and learned, and they change.
6. **Each Stage 2 valley is a chapter config** as in ADR 0013 item 9, with its scenario, scene, palette, keepsake (spiral shell, ballot chit, reed whistle), guide (Brume, Ambry, Pell), two or three postcards, and placeholder text and art (ADR 0010). Its local verbs: Foglight `stepDown`; Council `vote` (one tray tool: the four spots and, once, the three rules to propose); Shallows `sing` (one tray tool: learn from the picked frog, or sing to it).
7. **Sandbox modes (spec 13.1).** A finished Stage 2 valley is a sandbox valley like the Stage 1 ones (ADR 0014 item 1), with no hero; its knobs: "How far snails see" (`foglight.sight`, 1 to 3 steps), "Voting rule" (the council's rule, by name: plurality, approval, instant runoff, score), "Frogs want at least this many like them" (`shallows.minShare`, 0 to 0.5 in 0.05 steps; unset is 1/3), and "Frogs leave a pad this alike" (`shallows.maxShare` with the variety rule on from the start, 0.5 to 1 in 0.05 steps; unset leaves the variety rule to the songs). A knob with names shows its name, not its number.
8. **Rule cards (spec 13.1, 21.2).** The Rules panel holds up to `MAX_CARDS` (3) cards, each "When a creature is *when*, it *then*". *when* is a state the creature is in now, never who it is (spec 19.2): `hungry` (fewer berries than `gift.cost`), `full` (at least `forage.carryMax` berries), `lonely` (no friends), `liked` (at least 3 friends). *then* is a meeting move: `toward`, `away`, or `against` for its reply to a bid, `give` or `keep` for its gift. In a meeting, the first card whose *when* holds and whose *then* is a reply sets the replier's reply, and the first such gift card sets each side's gift; a gift card cannot give without the berries for it (`giftCost`). The hero's manner wins over a card; the draws are still taken (ADR 0003). A card is a scenario field (`cards`), read only when present, so the M0 golden hash does not move. A card change starts the run again, as a knob does.
9. **Travelers by code (spec 21.2).** In the sandbox the player picks a creature and asks for its traveler code: `t1.` and the URL-safe base64 of deflate-raw over the traveler's JSON, as a seed link is made. A traveler holds its home id (from the closed set of base creatures), its species, its dials, its shell and resting shell, its sky warmth, and its memories: its last diary lines as `{ key, other }` pairs (a `content/diary.json` key and a base creature id, or `hero`), at most 20. No free text, so no name or caption can ride in a code. "Welcome a traveler" adds the code's creature to the setup (at most `MAX_TRAVELERS`, 3): it arrives at the gate as `traveler-<n>` with its home name and its body, and its memories open its Soul diary. A traveler is part of the setup, so it is in the valley's seed link, and welcoming one starts the run again.
10. **The remix tree (spec 21.2).** A link holds `from`, the ids of the links it was remixed from, oldest first (at most `MAX_LINEAGE`, 16). A link's id is the first 8 hex digits of FNV-1a over its text. A run started from an opened link keeps that link's lineage plus its id, and a rule change keeps it; a new valley starts none. The device keeps the links the player made or opened (at most `MAX_KEPT_LINKS`, 30, oldest dropped) with their ids, lineage, valley, and day, in the profile, never sent anywhere. The Share panel's "Remix tree" lists them as a tree: a link under the newest kept link in its lineage, an unknown parent as "A valley someone else made", and each kept link with an "Open" button. Links without `from` (M4 links) stay valid.
11. **Hero balance** (ADR 0013 item 15) holds for each Stage 2 valley: for each hero kind over seeds 1 to 10 the T3 player brings the twist in at least 8 and the control in at most 2.
12. **T5 (spec 18).** Over seeds 1 to 50, Lily Shallows without a hero and with the variety rule off sorts: `sorting` rises from its start to at least `shallows.sortedAt` within 2 days in at least 45 seeds. With the variety rule on from the start in the same layout after sorting, `sorting` falls to at most `shallows.mixedAt` within 2 more days in at least 45.
13. **Save and link formats.** The new pack states, cards, travelers, and lineage are optional fields: a save of version 3 and an M4 link stay readable. The tuning hash changes with the new tuning blocks, so an M4 link made under the old tuning opens the broken-link page (ADR 0014 item 7).
14. **Foglight Heights calibration (M5.6).** The map: Brume's hill, a cone with its peak (5) at column 4, row 4; the fog valley at columns 6 to 8 (1, with a floor of 0 at column 7); and a slope east to the meadow (9) from column 13, rows 1 to 7 (the top and bottom rows rise only to 6). The gate stands on the hill's foot, so the hero climbs to Brume with no step down. `fogBelow` is 5, so the hilltop is clear: at 6, its fog misread gifts in 3 to 7 of 40 runs and kept Brume cold. Brume's bush (10 berries) stands beside its peak. The day is 360 s and the window 3 days. One meeting gives Brume at most 0.25 toward the hero, so it follows on the second day's meeting. The T3 player sets toward and give, steps down at once, waves at Brume every 20 ticks until it follows, then walks to the meadow: the twist in 200 of 200 runs, the postcard at 409 s (392 s for a Kite-rider). The control never steps down: 0 of 200.
15. **Toadstool Council calibration (M5.7).** The spots: Mossbank (0.2, 0.2), Rootstep (0.25, 0.25), Highstone (0.8, 0.8), and Dry hollow (0.8, 0.2); 8 voters near Mossbank (Ambry among them), 7 near Rootstep, and 10 near Highstone, each within 0.01. Plurality elects Highstone, approval and runoff Mossbank, and score Rootstep. The toadstools carry 5 berries, so none leaves in the valley's window. The day is 200 s and the window 3 days. The T3 player votes for Rootstep and proposes approval after the first tally: the twist at the second tally in 200 of 200 runs, the postcard at 400 s. The control never proposes: 0 of 200.
16. **Lily Shallows calibration (M5.8).** Six pads, 30 frogs, 5 to a pad, each pad mixed (two of one song, two of the next, one of the third); Pell sings the old song. `everyTicks` is 50, so a frog hops at most every 5 s. The hero teaches one song a day (`sangOn`; the snapshot's `sangToday`), so the three songs that turn the variety rule on take three days. A small pond does not always sort fully: with `minShare` 1/3 it rises from 0.2 to at least 0.84 in 47 of 50 seeds and stalls at 0.73 to 0.76 in the rest, so `sortedAt` is 0.8. With the variety rule on, it falls to at most 0.39 in 50 of 50, so `mixedAt` is 0.5. The day is 180 s and the window 3 days. The T3 player watches for a minute, learns Pell's song, and sings to one one-song frog of another song each day: the twist in 200 of 200 runs, the postcard at 375 s at the median. The control learns and never sings: 0 of 200.

## Consequences

- Each new rule is on only with its scenario field, so the M0 golden hash `c29d2fa4dbda4dcf` does not change.
- The homecoming's stored carry and its T3 run change with the six Kith legs.
- A fourth valley kind (a pack) costs one pack file, one config, one scenario, one scene, one palette, and its tests, as ADR 0013 promised.
